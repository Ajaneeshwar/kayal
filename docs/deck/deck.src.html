Kayal Log is a Malayalam voice logbook that lets Alappuzha houseboat crews prove where their sewage and waste went.

It is built for Malayalam speakers, and it hears and answers in the crew's own language: Malayalam, Tamil, Hindi, Bengali and more.

It is one solution covering all three HackaThoni tracks. I am Ajaneeshwar, and I built it during HackaThoni.

But only one of the two plants works. And there is no shared record of who emptied what.

Government estimates count about 700 registered houseboats and 750 unregistered ones. Inspectors check them one at a time. In April, dealing with one boat that had no valid papers took so long that the team could not check the others.

It hurts the crew, who earn their living on these boats. It hurts the lake. And a tourist has no way to check any of it.

Sources, if a judge asks: Down To Earth, 25 May 2026 for the boat estimate and the plants. Onmanorama, 21 Apr 2026 for the inspection. The boat count is an estimate, so do not give it as exact. Say "we found no existing tool", not "it is the only tool".

A crew member says it in their own language: Malayalam, Tamil, Hindi, Bengali or another. The phone checks its location against the known collection points. It reads a photo of the receipt.

The plant, truck, pump boat or collector that took the waste then approves the handover on their own phone, on the receiver page.

Proof levels

Every entry shows how strong its proof is.

Weakest to strongest
Declared
The crew said it. Nothing else confirms it.
Location
The phone was at an open collection point.
Receipt
A photo of the receipt or slip backs the entry.
Confirmed
The receiver confirmed it on their own phone.
Disputed
The receiver says it never arrived. The entry drops to Declared.
The crew adds a slip photo
The receiver confirms
Location shows the phone was there, not that the tank was emptied. A receipt photo is read, not checked against the plant's records.
Kayal Log · 5

Each entry gets a proof level: Declared, Location, or Receipt. Then the plant, truck or collector that took the waste confirms it on their own phone. That is Confirmed, the strongest level.

If the receiver says the waste never reached them, the entry is marked Disputed and drops to Declared.

Do not say location proves the tank was emptied. It proves the phone was there. Do not say a receipt is proof. Source: PLAN.md and README.md.

Kayal Log · 6

Two more checks. First, the receiver. The plant, truck, pump boat or collector that took the waste opens the receiver page on their own phone, scans the code on the crew's phone, and taps Confirm received or Not received. Received raises the entry to Confirmed. Not received marks it Disputed, and it drops to Declared.

Second, the reminder call. For a boat that is due or overdue, the inspector taps Call owner, and Sarvam Voice Agents phones the owner with a reminder in Malayalam or English.

If a judge asks: the receiver has 3 days to answer, and the answer cannot be changed later. In this build every reminder call goes to one configured number.

Kayal Log · 8

This slide can be skipped if time is short.

The crew's speech, in Malayalam, Tamil, Hindi or another Indian language, is turned into text by Sarvam Saaras v4. Gemma 4 reads the sentence, and reads the receipt photo. The phone's position is compared with the known collection points. That sets the proof level.

The reply is translated into the language the crew spoke, by Sarvam-Translate, and spoken by Bulbul v3. The receiver's answer raises the entry to Confirmed, or marks it Disputed. Tourists and inspectors see the record straight away.
